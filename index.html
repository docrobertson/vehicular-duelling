The Rules of the Road

  • Armor is tracked per side — front, back, left, right, top & under. Breach a side and hits chew into weapons, motor, battery, crew and tires.
  • Heat builds from lasers, heavy guns, boosting and fire. Overheat and big guns jam and your motor throttles back. Heat sinks help.
  • Battery powers your motor, boost and energy weapons. Run dry and you crawl.
  • A gunner auto-aims the turret and side guns, and takes the wheel if the driver goes down.
  • Ram with spikes for big damage. Speed & mass matter.
  • Grab glowing crates: green repairs, yellow re-arms, cyan cools & recharges.
  • Brackets: your car's total cost sets your division; opponents are built to the same budget.
